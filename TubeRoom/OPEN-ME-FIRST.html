TubeRoom 1.2.2

Double-click Start-TubeRoom.command, then open TubeRoom in Safari.

Connect your Mac and tubes to the same router. Setup → add tubes → Check & save. Choose a tube microphone and click Connect; compatible controllers may restart. Pick a group and an effect, then Start lights.

Sound capture and FFT run on WLED. Color loop → Big bass hits filters those levels on the Mac; Tune bass trigger lets you choose only the stronger booms. Sound-triggered color loops need the launcher running and the Mac awake; Safari can close. Other onboard effects continue without the Mac. Use Blackout to switch them off, or Stop & restore to return to the previous WLED state.

No firmware changes. See README.md for supported effects and limitations.
